OpenMantel

Your photos on every screen in the house.

Host your own See the demo GitHub

OpenMantel is a free, open source picture frame you host yourself. Put a small computer behind a monitor on the kitchen counter, plug it in, and your photos are on display. Store-bought frames do much the same, but they often lock you into an app, a subscription, or someone else's cloud.

What it does

  • Pairs like a Roku

    A frame shows a code, you enter it on your phone, and it never asks again. Frames need no keyboard and no password.

  • Runs from your phone

    Start a slideshow on every frame or just one, and skip, go back, or pause like a remote. Changes reach the frame instantly.

  • Never crops a photo

    Portrait, square, and panoramic photos all show in full, on a matte or a blurred backdrop, with optional captions.

  • Organizes by tag

    Upload in batches, straight from your phone's photo library, tag photos, and save presets like Holidays.

  • Builds playlists

    Drag photos into a playlist like Kitchen, in the order you want, and give each frame its own.

  • Plays forever

    In random order, every pass is freshly shuffled. In sequence, it follows your order, then starts again.

  • Keeps playing offline

    Each frame caches the library, so it keeps going without a network, even after a restart.

  • Takes iPhone photos

    HEIC works, photos are rotated correctly, and location data is removed from everything a frame receives.

  • Shows what's in each photo

    See any photo's format, size, compression, camera settings, and where it was taken, if the camera recorded it.

Yours, not a service

Run it on a small VPS or add it to your homelab. Your photos stay on your server, with no sign-ups and no tracking. It opens with a few sample photos so you can try it straight away, then switches to yours with your first upload. OpenMantel is MIT licensed and free, and it always will be.

It's part of the metaincognita suite of simulations and tools, and it stands entirely on its own.

Host your own

You need a server with Docker and a domain name. HTTPS is required, and the included setup gets the certificate for you.

git clone https://github.com/cschweda/openmantel.git
cd openmantel
cp .env.example .env && chmod 600 .env   # set DOMAIN
docker compose run --rm app node_modules/.bin/tsx scripts/hash-password.ts
# paste the printed line into .env
docker compose up -d

The self-hosting guide covers homelab setups, your own reverse proxy, updates, and backups.

Set up a frame

  1. Connect a Mac mini or other small computer to a monitor, and run its setup script once.
  2. The screen shows a code. Log in on your phone and enter it.
  3. Photos start by themselves. Put the keyboard away.
